PAYDAY — PRODUCT LISTING DRAFT
==============================

Suggested launch price: EUR 3.99 one-time purchase (introductory test price).
This is a suggested price, not a guarantee of sales or profit.

PRODUCT TITLE
Payday — Simple Budget Planner for Your Next Paycheck

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Know what you can safely spend today. Plan income, bills, everyday expenses and savings between paydays with a simple mobile-friendly budget app.

FULL DESCRIPTION (ENGLISH)
Your money has to last until payday. Payday helps you make a simple plan and see an estimated daily spending limit after your planned bills, logged spending and savings targets.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Mobile-friendly app that can be installed from a supported browser when hosted over HTTPS
- Five interface languages: English, Bulgarian, Spanish, French and German
- EUR, USD and GBP currency display
- Last payday and next payday settings
- Income and expense tracking
- Planned bills, including paid/unpaid status
- Savings goals and contributions
- Estimated available balance and daily spending limit
- Local data storage, with JSON backup and restore
- Basic offline app shell after the app has been installed and opened online

PLEASE READ BEFORE BUYING
- This is a personal budgeting tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.
- It does not connect to your bank or import transactions automatically.
- Your budget data is stored in the browser on your device; there is no cloud sync or user account.
- Clearing browser/site data may erase saved entries. Export backups regularly.
- The app requires an internet connection for its first visit. Offline behavior depends on browser support and successful installation.
- A purchase does not include a native App Store or Google Play listing.

PERSONAL USE LICENSE
One purchase grants the buyer a personal, non-transferable license to use the app. Redistribution, resale, sublicensing or sharing the product files publicly is not permitted.
